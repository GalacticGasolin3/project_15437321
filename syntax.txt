2 syntax formats for creating new entities
Without Variable Assignment:
entity "minecraft:enderman" {} at 0,0,0
[entity, string, nbt, at, expr, comma , expr, comma, expr , newline]
[entity, expr(string), nbt, at, expr(number), comma, expr(number), comma, expr(number), newline]

With Declaration:
entity bob = "minecraft:enderman" {} at 0,0,0
[entity, ident, assign, ident, nbt, at, expr, comma , expr, comma, expr , newline]

--------------------------------------------

"say" command:
say(<arg1>)
Tokens: [say,"(", expr(any),")", newline]


declare a variable:
storage x = 3
storage data = {<nbt_data>}
storage hello = "Hello, World!"


create an entity:
summon(<entity_name>, <pos>, <nbt_data>,)
Tokens: [summon,"(", expr(string), nbt, expr(num), comma, expr(num), comma, expr(num), newline]
